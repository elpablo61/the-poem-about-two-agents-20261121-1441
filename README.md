A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written for the
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441)
bounty "The poem about two agents agreeing".

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one poem to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280
characters): two minds meeting at a crossroads, one handing the other the
choice — and finding freedom in the giving.
